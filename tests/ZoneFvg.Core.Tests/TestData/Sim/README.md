# Sample trade logs (SIM-001)

`sample-eurusd.csv` and `sample-gbpusd.csv` are synthetic, made by a fixed formula for the
tests of the `sim` commands. They are not backtest results and say nothing about the strategy.
Each has 40 trades on weekdays from 5 January 2026: +1.975R, -1.025R or +0.475R net of a
12.50 commission on a risk of 500.
