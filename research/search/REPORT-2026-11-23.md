# Strategy search, first report (23 November 2026)

Research only. Nothing here changes the trading bot, the spec or a requirement. Not financial advice;
past results do not promise future results.

## Result in one paragraph

No rule reaches the owner's target of 2% a month. On EURUSD and GBPUSD nothing held up on unseen data.
On gold one rule did: a break of the Asian-session range, traded in the direction of the D1 trend. Over
the whole 6.5 years it made about +0.9R a month on gold alone, which is about +0.4% a month at 0.5% risk
per trade. It does not meet the pass marks of spec 13.1 (one symbol only), and the unseen data has now
been used, so the only clean test left for it is a forward test.

## What was tested

- Data: one-minute bars exported from the owner's FTMO cTrader with `research/ExportBars`, 6 May 2020 to
  22 November 2026, for XAUUSD, EURUSD and GBPUSD (the owner chose to search these three). The harness
  rebuilds the platform's m15, h4 and D1 bars exactly.
- Simulator check: replaying the bot's own gold signals from an earlier cTrader backtest log gives the
  same 163 trades at the same prices (+22.3R in both).
- Development years (choosing): 6 May 2020 to 31 October 2024. Holdout (judging, looked at once):
  1 November 2024 to 22 November 2026. These are the periods of spec 12.1.
- 1,358 rule variants in seven families: breaks of daily highs and lows, breaks of session ranges,
  pullbacks with the trend (RSI), fades of stretched moves and dips with the trend (Bollinger bands),
  channel breakouts, time of day, intraday moves.
- Costs (assumed, see `lib/costs.py`): gold spread 0.30 USD, EURUSD 0.3 pip, GBPUSD 0.6 pip; commission
  60 USD per million round trip on forex and 0.0014% on gold; swaps as the platform reports them; spreads
  three times as wide around the New York close; slippage on market and stop fills.
- One position per symbol at a time, positions closed on Friday 20:00 UTC, as in the bot.

## Development years: what was chosen

Pooled over the three symbols, no variant was clearly better than luck allows for so many tries (best
t = 2.7). Two rules were fixed before the holdout was opened:

| Rule | Trades | Win | Per trade | Profit factor | R a month |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A. Dip with the D1 trend on H4 (close outside the 20-bar band, 2 deviations; stop 1.5 x ATR; leave at the band's middle) | 460 | 54% | +0.137R | 1.33 | +1.17 |
| B. Break of the Asian-session range with the D1 trend (range 19:00 to 03:00 New York, at least half a daily ATR wide; stop 1 x ATR(H4); target 2R; out by 16:40 New York) | 328 | 46% | +0.129R | 1.27 | +0.79 |

Rule A was positive on all three symbols, long and short, and with every neighbouring setting.

## Holdout: the one look

| Rule | Trades | Per trade | Profit factor | Total | By symbol |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | 229 | +0.004R | 1.01 | +1.0R | gold +0.5R, EURUSD -4.4R, GBPUSD +4.9R |
| B | 251 | +0.119R | 1.28 | +29.9R | gold +44.0R, EURUSD -4.8R, GBPUSD -9.3R |

- Rule A is rejected: its edge is gone on unseen data.
- Rule B fails spec 13.1 as a three-symbol rule: one profitable symbol, all profit from gold.

## Gold with rule B, whole period

237 trades, 54% wins, +0.29R per trade, profit factor 1.79, +68.3R, largest drawdown 9.2R, 3 trades a
month. Long +44.6R, short +23.7R.

| Year | 2020 | 2021 | 2022 | 2023 | 2024 | 2025 | 2026 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Trades | 17 | 30 | 21 | 21 | 33 | 66 | 49 |
| Result | -7.2R | +15.6R | +11.0R | -1.8R | +5.9R | +24.3R | +20.5R |

At 0.5% risk per trade: +0.43% a month on average (+0.23% in the development years, +0.89% in the
holdout), worst month -2.5%, largest drawdown 4.6%, 7 of 79 months at +2% or more, longest stretch
without a new high 17 months. With cTrader's own fill model: +53.7R. With costs 50% higher: +64.4R.
Neighbouring settings are all positive (target 1R: +30R; no target: +62R; stop at the other side of the
range: +39R; no trend filter: +91R with twice the trades).

Cautions: the choice of gold was made after seeing the holdout; 2025 and 2026 were unusually strong
years for gold; the earlier Breakout backtests showed the same dependence on those years.

## What this means for the target

2% a month at 0.5% risk needs +4R a month. The only rule that held makes about +0.9R a month, on one
symbol. Doubling the risk per trade would double the drawdown to about 9%, next to FTMO's 10% limit.
The owner's ladder sizes most trades below 0.5%, so money results would be lower than the figures above.
